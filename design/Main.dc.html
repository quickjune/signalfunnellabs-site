01 / The leak

Your ad did its job. The page after it is where the money leaks.

Ad platforms get a visitor to your door. What the visitor sees in the next few seconds decides whether that spend becomes revenue or a line on a report. These are the four places it usually goes wrong.

LEAK 01

A first screen that loads slowly on a phone

Most paid clicks arrive on mobile. If the page is still painting, the visitor is already back on the results page, and you paid for the visit anyway.

LEAK 02

An ad that promises one thing and a page that opens with another

The visitor clicked for a specific offer. A generic homepage makes them hunt for it, and most will not.

LEAK 03

Too many doors and no obvious winner

A full navigation bar, five competing buttons and a popup. Every extra choice gives the visitor a reason not to take the one that earns.

LEAK 04

A last step that does not close the sale

Contact forms that go nowhere, booking flows that dead-end, checkouts that ask for too much too early. The interest was there. The path was not.
